# S002: explicit-sync

Explicit CUDA synchronization may limit asynchronous execution.

## Trigger and boundaries

A call resolves to torch.cuda.synchronize, including import aliases. Severity is high in a repeated context and medium elsewhere. Shadowed names and Event/Stream methods are excluded. Timing and correctness may require synchronization.

## Explanation
